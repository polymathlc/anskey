Worked answer

Answer: (1)

In circuit (1), bulb P is connected in parallel with the other two bulbs. It is on a branch of its own, so it gets the full push of the 2 batteries. In circuits (2), (3) and (4), bulb P is connected in series with at least one other bulb, so the push of the batteries is shared and less current flows through bulb P. The more electric current flows through a bulb, the brighter it is, so bulb P is brightest in circuit (1).

    Key takeaway

    The more electric current flows through a bulb, the brighter it glows.

    • More batteries give a bigger push, so more current flows.
    • Bulbs in series share the push of the batteries, so each one gets less current.
    • A bulb in parallel, on a branch of its own, gets the full push of the batteries.
