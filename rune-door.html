P5 Science — Heat and Temperature

Polymath Learning Centre · Worksheet 7

  1. Why does a metal spoon feel colder than a wooden spoon at the same temperature? Metal is a better conductor of heat, so it takes heat from the hand faster.
  2. Ice cubes were placed in a glass of water. Explain what happens to the ice.
  3. State one way to reduce heat loss from a cup of hot soup.
